## Annex B: NTL of Urban Areas
All of the urban graphs below have the logarithm of NTL radiance plotted. The legends are relative to the maximum logged radiance value in that urban area across time, care should therefore be taken when comparing the graphs of cities. 

### Hargeisa
Hargeisa shows a dramatic urban expansion from 2012-2023. The number of lit pixels in the city have increased from 324 in 2012 to 965 in 2023. However, it is unknown to what extent the expansion is actually just previously unlit areas of Hargeisa accessing electricity. Nevertheless, when using NTL as a proxy for economic activity it shows a dramatic expansion in Hargeisa. Even with the dramatic increase in the extensive margin, the increase in the intensity of radiance emitted is proportionally more, a 375% increase in intensity compared to a 198% increase in lit pixels. Interestingly, the ratio of the increase in intensive to extensive margin is lower for Hargeisa than for all other cities except Boroma which has the same ratio.

### Berbera
Berbera shows a more dramatic growth in NTL emissions on both the intensive and extensive margin than Hargeisa. In 2012, the number of lit pixels was 69, by 2023 this had increased to 331. However, the growth in the radiance of pixels (796%) has increased even more dramatically than the growth in the number of pixels (380%). The brightest part of Somaliland can be found in the port. The growth appears to be driven by the expansion of the Port, with new industries and businesses appearing to take advantage of the improved infrastructure.

### Burao
Burao has expanded dramatically, however it still remains smaller both on the intensive and extensive margin than Hargeisa did in 2012. The number of lit pixels has increased from 86 to 234 following a similar scale of expansion as Hargeisa. It is important to note that Burao is one of the most important cities for livestock in Somaliland and therefore it is likely that more economic activity remains uncaptured here than elsewhere. Nevertheless, we would expect that increased economic growth would have translated to increased electrification through greater incomes. The increase in the intensive margin increased much faster than the increase in the extensive margin, with the radiance increasing by 415% compared to only a 172% increase in the number of lit pixels. In Burao the proportionate increase in the extensive margin to the intensive margin was greater than all other cities apart from Erigavo. This implies that the existing areas of electrification have gotten brighter faster than urban expansion/expansion of access to electricity.

### Boroma
Boroma has grown dramatically, from 40 pixels in 2012 to 177 in 2023. The intensive and extensive margins are still smaller than that of Hargeisa but there has been a much greater increase in the extensive margin. The proportional increase in radiance to lit pixels remains high, however this ratio is lower than for any other city in this sample apart from Hargeisa. This implies that Boroma has jointly faced the greatest expansion in access to electricity/urban expansion compared to increasing brightness in existing areas.

### Erigavo
Erigavo is the main city in the Sanaag region and remains smaller than any other comparable city apart from Las Anood in the Sool region. The number of lit pixels in 2012 was just 11 and this has expanded to 68 in 2023. This small increase demonstrates how this area has had very little increase in access to electricity and that economic activity remains informal and based on agriculture and pastoralism.

### Las Anood
Las Anood is an incredibly interesting example since conflict erupted in 2023 between government forces and the Dhulbahante clan there. Previously Las Anood helped to facilitate trade between Somalia and Somaliland. However since the conflict started this trade has stopped. The impact of the conflict can be seen in the NTL emissions of the city. Pixels increased from 11 in 2012 to 85 in 2022, before dropping to 66 in 2023. Radiance faced a more dramatic drop after the conflict started, from 11.8 in 2012, to 227.6 in 2022, before dropping to 138.1 in 2023. The city is now believed to be under the control of Al-Shabaab.
